========================================================
             WILD SENTINEL V0.9
       COMPETING HYPOTHESES ENGINE
========================================================

ADVERSARIAL TEST

The leopard initially travels diagonally.

At approximately 20 minutes it changes behaviour
while the direct sensor is blind.

Wild Sentinel does NOT know which behaviour occurred.

Instead of maintaining one trajectory, the engine
must maintain competing hypotheses:

    H1  CONTINUE ORIGINAL TRAJECTORY
    H2  CHANGE DIRECTION
    H3  SLOW / STOP
    H4  UNKNOWN MOVEMENT

Each hypothesis has:

    Position estimate
    Velocity estimate
    Direction
    Uncertainty
    Confidence
    Evidence support
    Probability weight

========================================================

CORE PRINCIPLE

Wild Sentinel must NOT ask:

    "Where is the leopard?"

It must ask:

    "Where could the leopard plausibly be?"

and then:

    "Which human routes remain safe
     across those plausible futures?"

========================================================
                 V0.9 PRINCIPLE
========================================================

V0.8:

    "Something may have changed."

V0.9:

    "There are several plausible explanations
     for what may have changed."

Therefore Wild Sentinel maintains:

       H1 ────────────────┐
                          │
       H2 ────────────────┼──> FUTURE RISK
                          │
       H3 ────────────────┤
                          │
       H4 ────────────────┘

Instead of:

       ONE PREDICTION
            ↓
       ONE ROUTE DECISION


========================================================

CRITICAL SAFETY RULE

A route is NOT SAFE merely because:

       "the most probable hypothesis
        says it is safe."

A route can only be considered SAFE when:

       the plausible dangerous hypotheses
       have sufficiently low risk,

       AND

       uncertainty is sufficiently low.


========================================================

NEXT: V0.9.1
========================================================

V0.9.1 should introduce:

       HYPOTHESIS MEMORY

The engine should remember:

    which hypotheses were correct
    which failed
    how quickly behaviour changed
    how long animals remained on altered paths
    which indirect signals preceded changes

This creates the beginning of:

       BEHAVIOURAL MEMORY

rather than simply trajectory prediction.

And that is where Wild Sentinel starts becoming
something much more interesting than a tracking system.
